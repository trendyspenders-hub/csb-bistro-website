Where to Watch UFC in Vancouver

Pay-per-view fight nights, UFC Fight Nights, and boxing main events. Find the best bar to catch every round.

UFC nights are different from other sports. The energy builds all night, the crowd reacts to every exchange, and a single moment can change the entire fight. If you want to experience that with other fans, you need a bar that takes combat sports seriously. CSB Bistro & Sport Bar on Granville Street is one of the best places in Vancouver to watch the UFC.

Big Screens and Full Sound

The most important thing for a UFC watch party is the AV setup. You want big screens, clear picture, and sound loud enough that you hear every corner instruction, every commentary call, and every reaction from the crowd. CSB has multiple screens and a sound system that makes the main card feel like the main event.

Why UFC Fans Choose CSB

The crowd matters for fight nights. Watching alone at home is fine for a prelim, but a pay-per-view main event is better with people who understand the stakes. CSB draws a mix of hardcore fans and casual viewers, which makes the room feel electric when a fight delivers.

The location also helps. Granville Street is easy to reach by transit, and there are plenty of parking options nearby. That matters when the main card starts late and you do not want to deal with a long commute home.

Food and Drinks for Fight Night

Fight nights are long. The early prelims can start before most dinner services, and the main event might not happen until after 10 PM. CSB's menu is built for that kind of night. Wings, nachos, burgers, and shareable plates keep you fed, and the drink menu covers beer, cocktails, wine, and highballs.

If you are there during happy hour, which runs from 3PM to 6PM daily, you can take advantage of $10 appetizers, $5 drinks, and $8 doubles before the main card begins.

Reserve a Table for UFC Pay-Per-Views

The biggest UFC events fill up fast. If you want a guaranteed seat for a title fight or a stacked pay-per-view, booking a table in advance is the smart move. CSB takes reservations by phone or through the contact form.
