The Ultimate Sports Season Is Here

What to watch at CSB from July through September 2026, including CFL, Whitecaps, Formula 1, MLB and the NFL.

From CFL football and Vancouver Whitecaps soccer to Formula 1, golf, MLB and the return of the NFL, the next three months are packed with must-watch sporting action. Find your favourite games, bring your crew and make sure you book your spot at CSB Bistro • Sport Bar.

Summer is heating up, and so is the sports calendar.

From late July through the end of September, Vancouver sports fans have an incredible lineup of games and events to look forward to. Whether you're cheering for the BC Lions, following the Vancouver Whitecaps, watching the CFL, keeping up with MLB, or counting down to the return of the NFL, there is always something worth watching.

Add in Formula 1, major golf tournaments, the Vancouver Canadians and a packed September schedule, and you have the perfect excuse to get together with friends, grab some great food and enjoy the action on the big screens at CSB Bistro • Sport Bar.

With so many major games happening throughout the summer and into the fall, there is one important piece of advice:

Make sure to book your table in advance to guarantee your spot for your favourite sports and biggest games.

Popular matchups and major sporting events can bring in crowds, and the best seats can go quickly. If there is a game you absolutely don't want to miss, planning ahead is the best way to make sure you and your group have a place to enjoy it.

Here's your guide to the biggest sports events coming to the calendar from July through September 2026.

🏈 July: The CFL Season Is Heating Up

The summer sports calendar gets rolling with two exciting BC Lions games in July.

BC Lions vs. Toronto Argonauts

Saturday, July 25 -- 4:00 PM

The Toronto Argonauts visit the BC Lions at BC Place, giving local football fans an excellent opportunity to gather with friends and enjoy an afternoon of CFL action.

For BC Lions fans, home games always bring a special energy, and watching the game surrounded by fellow supporters makes the experience even better.

Grab your friends, order some food and drinks, settle in and cheer on the Lions.

If you're planning to make a full afternoon of it, consider booking ahead so your group has a spot ready for kickoff.

BC Lions at Winnipeg Blue Bombers

Thursday, July 30 -- 5:30 PM

The Lions head east to take on the Winnipeg Blue Bombers at Princess Auto Stadium.

A Thursday evening game is the perfect opportunity to meet up after work, enjoy dinner and catch the action.

It's also a great reminder that you don't have to wait until the weekend to get together.

Sometimes, a midweek football game is exactly what you need to break up the workweek.

If you're planning to watch this one with a group, book ahead to guarantee your spot and make sure you're ready for kickoff.

🏎️ Formula 1: Hungarian Grand Prix

August 2-4

Formula 1 fans can turn their attention to the Hungarian Grand Prix at the Hungaroring.

F1 is about more than just speed. Strategy, tire management, overtaking and championship pressure can all play a role in determining the outcome.

Watching a Grand Prix with friends is a great way to enjoy the race while sharing the excitement of every lap.

If Formula 1 is your thing, keep an eye on the CSB sports schedule and make plans to join us for the biggest race weekends.
