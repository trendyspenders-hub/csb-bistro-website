Best Happy Hour Downtown Vancouver

A local guide to finding solid happy hour deals on Granville Street, including daily $10 appetizers and $5 drinks at CSB.

Granville Street is packed with places to grab a drink, but not every happy hour is worth your time. If you want a spot with good food, cold drinks, and a downtown location that is easy to reach, CSB Bistro & Sport Bar should be on your list. Our happy hour runs daily from 3PM to 6PM, and the deals are straightforward: $10 appetizers, $5 drinks, and $8 doubles.

What Makes a Great Happy Hour?

A good happy hour is about more than cheap beer. You want a menu that is actually worth eating, drinks that are mixed properly, and a space where you can sit down without fighting through a crowd. CSB checks those boxes. The food menu includes wings, nachos, burgers, and share plates that work whether you are stopping in for a snack or making dinner out of it.

Granville Street Location

CSB is at 1033 Granville Street, right in the Granville Entertainment District. That puts us a short walk from Granville Station and close to street parking and nearby parkades. Whether you are coming from the office, meeting friends, or starting a night out downtown, the location is hard to beat.

Daily Happy Hour, No Exceptions

Some bars limit happy hour to weekdays or make you guess what is included. At CSB, happy hour is simple. It runs every single day from 3PM to 6PM. The menu covers appetizers, drinks, and doubles, so you can order what you actually want.
