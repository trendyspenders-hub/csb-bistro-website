Where to Watch the Super Bowl in Vancouver

The biggest game of the year deserves a big crowd, big screens, and a bar that treats football like the main event.

The Super Bowl is not just a football game. It is a full evening of food, commercials, halftime, and the kind of crowd energy you cannot replicate at home. If you are looking for the best place to watch the Super Bowl in Vancouver, CSB Bistro & Sport Bar on Granville Street is built for nights like this.

Why Watch the Super Bowl at a Sports Bar?

At home, you are in charge of the remote, the snacks, and the cleanup. At a sports bar, you get to focus on the game. You get multiple screens, full sound, a menu you do not have to cook, and a room full of people who react to every play like you do.

The Super Bowl is better with a crowd. The commercials are funnier. The halftime show feels bigger. And when the game comes down to one drive in the fourth quarter, you want to be somewhere the whole room is holding its breath.

Big Screens and Full Sound

CSB is set up for viewing. Screens are positioned throughout the space so you never have to crane your neck to follow the action, and the sound is tuned for the game, not background noise. Whether you are there for the football, the halftime show, or both, you will not miss a moment.

Game-Day Food and Drinks

Super Bowl Sunday calls for shareable food. Our menu has wings, nachos, burgers, poutine, and plenty of other options that are easy to pass around a table. Pair that with beer, wine, highballs, or cocktails, and you have everything you need for a four-quarter night.

Book Your Table Early

Super Bowl Sunday fills up fast everywhere, and CSB is no exception. If you are planning to watch with a group, it is worth reserving a table ahead of time. For larger parties, ask about reserving a section or hosting a private watch party.

Ready for Super Bowl Sunday?

Reserve your spot at CSB for the biggest NFL game of the year. Big screens, full sound, great food, and a crowd that loves football.

Watch NFL at CSB

CSB Bistro & Sport Bar is located at 1033 Granville Street in downtown Vancouver. Open Monday through Thursday from 12PM to 12AM, Friday and Saturday from 12PM to 1AM, and Sunday from 12PM to 10PM. For upcoming events and watch parties, visit our events page.
