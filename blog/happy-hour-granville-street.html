The Best Happy Hours on Granville Street

A local's take on where to find the strongest deals between 3PM and 5PM in downtown Vancouver.

Granville Street is not exactly known for cheap drinks. Most nights, a cocktail downtown will cost you somewhere between fourteen and eighteen dollars, and that is before tip. But if you know when to show up, the strip has a handful of happy hours that make an afternoon pint or an early dinner feel like a genuine win.

The timing matters. Downtown Vancouver happy hours tend to run early, usually between 3PM and 6PM, and the best spots do not just discount the drinks. They also cut prices on food, which is where you actually save money. A five dollar beer is nice. A five dollar beer with a ten dollar plate of nachos is how you turn one round into a full meal.

CSB Bistro & Sport Bar runs its happy hour daily from 3PM to 6PM. All appetizers are ten dollars, all drinks are five dollars, and doubles are eight dollars. That is a serious deal for Granville Street, especially when you factor in the location. The bar sits at 1033 Granville, right in the middle of the entertainment district, so it is an easy stop after work, before a show, or whenever you want to avoid the evening rush.

The food menu during happy hour covers the classics. Poutine, calamari, loaded nachos, and wings all show up at the discounted price. The drinks include beer, wine, and high-balls, so you are not stuck with a narrow list of well-options nobody wants. If you are the type who likes a double, the eight dollar price point is hard to beat anywhere nearby.

There are other decent happy hours on Granville, mostly at hotel bars and chain restaurants closer to Georgia Street. Some offer wine by the glass, others focus on draft beer. A few have food specials that rotate by the day. They are worth knowing about, but most close out by 5PM or 6PM and the discounts rarely cover both food and drinks at the same level.

The real advantage at CSB is consistency. The deal is the same every single day. You do not need to check a social media story or ask a server what is on special. You walk in between 3PM and 5PM, order what you want, and pay the happy hour price. That kind of predictability is rare downtown.

If you are planning a group outing, happy hour is also the smartest window. You can reserve a table, split a few appetizers, and keep the bill reasonable before the night picks up. The bar has plenty of screens too, so if there is an afternoon game on, you get the sports bar atmosphere without the late-night cover charge or the packed crowd.

Granville Street has plenty of places to spend money. The trick is knowing where to spend less of it. For a solid happy hour with real food, real drinks, and a location that actually makes sense, CSB's daily 3PM to 6PM window is one of the better options on the strip.

Daily happy hour at CSB Bistro & Sport Bar runs from 3PM to 6PM with $10 appetizers, $5 drinks, and $8 doubles.
