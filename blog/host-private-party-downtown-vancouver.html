Great Food for Every Guest

One of the biggest challenges when organizing a private event is making sure everyone has something they will enjoy.

At CSB, our menu is designed to offer plenty of variety.

Start your event with shareable appetizers such as poutine, loaded nachos, guacamole and chips, crispy calamari, chicken wings, spicy chicken bites or potato skins.

For guests looking for something more substantial, the menu includes burgers and sandwiches, pastas, mains, salads and bowls.

From classic comfort food to lighter options, your guests can choose the dishes that suit their tastes.

And when you're hosting a group, variety is key.

Some guests might want a burger.

Others may prefer pasta.

Someone else might be in the mood for fish and chips, tacos or a fresh salad.

Having a menu with plenty of options makes it easier to accommodate different preferences while keeping the entire group together.

Drinks to Keep the Celebration Going

What's a celebration without something great to drink?

CSB offers a wide selection of options to suit different tastes and occasions.

Guests can enjoy cocktails, beer, wine and highballs, along with non-alcoholic choices.

Whether your group is looking for a casual drink with dinner or you're planning a full evening of celebration, having a variety of beverage options ensures everyone can find something they enjoy.

And for groups looking to start their event earlier in the afternoon, CSB's happy hour offers another opportunity to enjoy the restaurant's food and drinks in a relaxed setting.

Happy hour runs from 3PM to 6PM, featuring $10 appetizers, $5 beer, wine and highballs, and $8 doubles.

For a corporate group or friends meeting after work, it can be a great way to start an evening together.
