Top Sports Bars in Vancouver

A local guide to the best places to watch sports in Vancouver, and why CSB Bistro & Sport Bar belongs on your list.

Vancouver has plenty of bars, but not all of them are set up for serious sports fans. If you are looking for a place where the game is the main event, the screens are easy to see, and the sound is actually on, CSB Bistro & Sport Bar on Granville Street is worth a visit.

What Makes a Great Sports Bar?

A great sports bar needs more than one TV in the corner. You want multiple screens so you can follow more than one game. You want sound, so the commentary and crowd noise are part of the experience. And you want food and drinks that keep you there from kickoff, tip-off, or puck drop until the end.

CSB delivers on all of that. The space is set up for viewing, with screens throughout the bar and sound that follows the biggest matchups. The menu covers bistro classics, share plates, burgers, and wings, and the drink list has beer, wine, cocktails, and doubles.

Granville Street Location

Location matters when you are meeting a group. CSB sits at 1033 Granville Street in the heart of downtown Vancouver. Transit, parking, and the rest of the nightlife strip are all within easy reach, which makes it a practical choice for any game day.

Happy Hour Every Day

Our daily happy hour from 3PM to 6PM is one of the reasons CSB stands out. $10 appetizers, $5 drinks, and $8 doubles make it easy to show up early and make a night of it. Check the sports schedule to see what is playing next.
