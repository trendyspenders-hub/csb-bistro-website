Where to Watch NFL Games in Vancouver

Sunday football, Monday Night Football, playoffs, and the Super Bowl. Here is where to find the best atmosphere in the city.

NFL fans in Vancouver know the struggle. Kickoff times rarely line up with dinner plans, and finding a bar that takes football seriously can be harder than it should be. If you are looking for a place where the screens are big, the sound is on, and the crowd actually watches the game, CSB Bistro & Sport Bar on Granville Street is worth a visit.

What Makes a Great NFL Bar?

A good sports bar for football needs more than one TV in the corner. You want multiple screens so you can catch the early games, the late games, and RedZone all at once. You want sound, so you hear the calls, the hits, and the crowd reactions. And you want food and drinks that keep you there from the opening kickoff through the final whistle.

CSB checks those boxes. The space is set up for viewing, with screens throughout the bar and sound that follows the biggest games. The menu is built for sharing, with wings, nachos, burgers, and poutine that are easy to split across a table.

Seattle Seahawks Fans Welcome

Vancouver has no shortage of Seahawks fans, and CSB is a natural home for them. The Seahawks are the closest NFL team to the city, and their games draw a crowd that knows the roster, follows the playoff picture, and stays locked in for all four quarters. If you want to watch the Seahawks with other fans, this is the spot.

Happy Hour Before Kickoff

Early games start at 10 AM Pacific, and afternoon games kick off at 1 PM or 5 PM. If you are settling in for the late afternoon slate, CSB's happy hour runs from 3PM to 6PM daily. That means $10 appetizers, $5 drinks, and $8 doubles while you wait for the next game to start.

Super Bowl and Playoffs

The NFL playoffs and Super Bowl are the biggest nights of the football calendar. CSB books up fast for those games, so reservations are a good idea. If you have a group, ask about reserving a section or planning a private watch party.

Ready for Game Day?

Check the full NFL schedule at CSB and book your table for the next Seahawks game or primetime matchup.

Watch NFL at CSB

CSB Bistro & Sport Bar is located at 1033 Granville Street in downtown Vancouver. Open Monday through Thursday from 12PM to 12AM, Friday and Saturday from 12PM to 1AM, and Sunday from 12PM to 10PM.
