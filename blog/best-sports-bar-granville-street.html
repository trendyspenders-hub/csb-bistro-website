Best Sports Bar on Granville Street

Big screens, cold drinks, game-day food, and a location that actually makes sense. Here is why CSB stands out.

Granville Street has no shortage of places to grab a drink, but not every bar is built for watching sports. If you want a place where the game is treated like the main event, you need more than a few TVs and a beer list. You need screens you can actually see, sound you can hear, food worth ordering, and a crowd that cares about the score. CSB Bistro & Sport Bar delivers on all of that.

Screens and Sound

The AV setup is the first thing that separates a real sports bar from a bar that happens to have a TV. CSB has multiple big screens throughout the space, so no matter where you sit, you have a clear view of the action. The sound is dialed in for the games that matter, whether it is a Canucks playoff game, a Whitecaps rivalry match, or a UFC pay-per-view.

Food Worth Showing Up For

A sports bar is only as good as the food that keeps you there. CSB's menu goes beyond basic pub fare. Wings, nachos, poutine, burgers, pasta, and mains are all made to share or to eat on your own. The portions are solid, the quality is consistent, and the prices are reasonable for Granville Street.

During happy hour, which runs daily from 3PM to 6PM, all appetizers are $10, all drinks are $5, and doubles are $8. That is one of the better deals you will find in the entertainment district.

A Central Location

CSB is at 1033 Granville Street, right in the middle of the Granville Entertainment District. It is a short walk from Granville Station, and there is street parking and parkades nearby. Whether you are coming from work, meeting friends, or heading out before a show, the location is hard to beat.

Events and Watch Parties

Beyond the daily sports schedule, CSB hosts watch parties for the biggest events of the year. Canucks games, Whitecaps matches, UFC fights, NFL Sundays, and the Super Bowl all draw a crowd. If you have a group, you can also book a private event or reserve a section.
