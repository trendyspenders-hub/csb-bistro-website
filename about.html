About CSB

A Bistro, A Sport Bar, A Local

We took over a familiar Granville Street pub and turned it into the kind of place where you can eat a real burger, watch the game, and stay for the music.

The Space

Same Address, New Energy

Before CSB, this was Crystal Sports Bar. Regulars had their stools, the beer was cold, and everyone knew the score. It was honest, unpretentious, and very much a neighbourhood spot.

When we took over, we didn't want to erase that. We wanted to keep the local feel and give people more reasons to show up. Better food on the plate. Better drinks in the glass. Screens you can actually see from the bar. A wine list that isn't an afterthought.

Now CSB is a bistro when you want dinner, a sport bar when the game is on, and a local when you just want a pint and a familiar face. From Wednesday to Saturday, DJ Shy Boy Steve and friends take over the decks and turn the room up.

We kept the bones of Crystal Sports Bar and built something worth coming back for.
