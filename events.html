Off-Site & In-House

Catering & Group Dining

Not every event needs to happen at our place. We can package up burgers, wings, salads, and shareable appetizers for pickup, ready to serve at your office, home, or venue.

If you would rather keep the group downtown, we can reserve a long table or section of the bar, build a set menu, and make sure nobody waits too long for a refill.

From finger food to full plated dinners, we can build a package that fits your group and your budget.
