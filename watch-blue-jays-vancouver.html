The Experience

Why Watch Blue Jays Games at CSB?

Watching the Toronto Blue Jays is better with a crowd. At CSB Bistro & Sport Bar on Granville Street, you get big screens throughout the bar, full game sound, and an atmosphere that makes every home run feel bigger.

Whether it is a regular-season game or playoff baseball, our downtown location makes it easy to meet up with friends, grab dinner, and stay late. Happy hour runs daily from 3PM to 6PM.
